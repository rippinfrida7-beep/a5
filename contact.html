Observatory FAQ

Gnomonics & Field Inquiries

Can a sundial be used in both Northern and Southern Hemispheres?

A standard horizontal sundial must have its hour lines mirrored and its gnomon angled toward the South Celestial Pole (True South) when installed in the Southern Hemisphere.

What is the best material for outdoor desert sundials?

Dense natural sandstone or engraved architectural bronze. Both materials withstand intense UV radiation, extreme temperature fluctuations ($20^\circ\text{F}$ to $120^\circ\text{F}$), and abrasive windblown sand without losing geometric calibration.
