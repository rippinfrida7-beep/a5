Cookie Policy & Browser Preferences

Effective Date: Autumn 2026 • Compliance: Global Web Standards

This Cookie Policy explains how CosmicDialDune uses cookies, browser local storage tokens, and related web technologies when you browse our educational site at https://cosmicdialdune.com. We are committed to transparency regarding how web data is collected and maintained on your devices.

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your computer or mobile device by a web server. Local storage is an HTML5 browser capability that enables websites to save user preference data directly within your client browser. These technologies allow our site to recognize your browsing device between visits, remembering interface preferences such as your chosen theme or typography scale without requiring manual re-selection on every page.

2. Categories of Storage Employed by CosmicDialDune

CosmicDialDune utilizes three specific types of client storage:

  • Functional & Preference Storage: Saves your chosen visual theme (Midnight Cosmos, Desert Twilight, or Dawn Sand) and in-article typography sizing settings.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Anonymous telemetry (Google Analytics G-0LY0HY7L01) used to monitor server latency, track popular celestial guides, and identify technical rendering errors.
  • Session State Tokens: Temporary memory keys that support smooth transitions between interactive solar calculators and masterclass treatises.

3. Local Storage vs. Tracking Cookies

CosmicDialDune prioritizes client-side HTML5 Local Storage over intrusive third-party tracking cookies. Local storage data stays entirely within your local browser sandbox, is never broadcasted to external advertising exchanges, and persists securely until you choose to clear your browser cache.

4. How to Manage and Disable Cookies

You retain full authority to configure cookie permissions in your browser preferences. All major browsers (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, Microsoft Edge) provide settings to block cookies, delete cached records, or operate in private incognito browsing modes. Disabling local storage may cause your visual theme settings to revert to the default theme upon page reload.
