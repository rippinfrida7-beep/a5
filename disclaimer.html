Safety Advisory

Horological & Solar Safety Disclaimer

Effective Date: Autumn 2026 • Scope: Solar Dials & Mechanical Care

The watchmaking treatises, solar sundial mathematics, astrolabe guides, and technical calibres published on CosmicDialDune are provided exclusively for informational, educational, and artistic appreciation.

1. Solar Observation Direct Eye Safety Warning

NEVER look directly at the Sun with the naked eye or through magnifying optical loupes, binoculars, or telescopes without ISO 12312-2 certified solar filters. When using solar sundial complications or gnomons, always observe the projected shadow on the dial face—never sight directly toward the solar disc.

2. Mechanical Watch Care & Water Resistance

Mechanical timepieces contain microscopic balance wheels and delicate jewel bearings. Avoid subjecting mechanical watches to intense magnetic fields (such as MRI machines or heavy industrial induction coils) unless your timepiece is equipped with a paramagnetic silicon escapement. Ensure screw-down crowns are fully secured before exposure to water.

3. Educational Nature of Astronomical Calculators

While our solar hour angle and azimuth simulator is derived from standard astronomical algorithms, it is designed for horological calibration and education and must not be used as the sole navigation instrument in remote wilderness.
