Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Autumn 2026 • Last Updated: 2026 Manufacture Edition • Jurisdiction: United States & International

At CosmicDialDune, accessible from https://cosmicdialdune.com, the privacy of our patrons, collectors, and visitors is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the specific types of technical and voluntarily submitted information gathered by CosmicDialDune and how that information is utilized.

1. Information We Collect

When you explore CosmicDialDune, our web servers collect standard non-personally identifiable log information, including your device IP address, browser type, date/time stamps, and page interactions. When you submit an inquiry through our concierge desk, we collect the personal identification details you provide, such as your full name, email address, and timepiece consultation details.

2. How We Utilize Collected Information

The information gathered is used strictly to respond to bespoke timepiece inquiries, manage atelier appointments, analyze traffic patterns across our horological journals, and optimize site performance. We never sell, monetize, or distribute personal collector records to commercial third-party marketing entities.

CosmicDialDune employs Google Analytics (Google Tag ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) to evaluate anonymous reader traffic, page load performance, and browser compatibility. These metrics are processed in aggregate form and do not link to personally identifiable identities.

Under GDPR and CCPA, all visitors possess statutory rights to request copies of their personal correspondence records, request corrections, or request complete data deletion.
