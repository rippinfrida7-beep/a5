Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Autumn 2026 • Last Updated: 2026 Edition • Jurisdiction: United States & International

At CosmicDialDune, accessible from https://cosmicdialdune.com, the privacy and digital sovereignty of our visitors is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the specific types of technical and voluntarily submitted information that is gathered and recorded by CosmicDialDune, and how that information is utilized to support our educational archaeoastronomy guides and celestial horology publications.

1. Information We Collect

When you explore CosmicDialDune, our web servers collect standard non-personally identifiable log information, including your device IP address, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click paths. When you contact our observatory desk via our online contact form, we collect the personal identification details you explicitly submit, such as your full name, email address, and message inquiry details.

2. How We Utilize Collected Information

The information gathered from site visitors is used strictly to operate, maintain, and enhance our digital publication. Specifically, aggregate data enables us to analyze reading patterns across our solar gnomonics masterclasses, improve interactive solar calculator tools, fix broken web links, and respond promptly to direct academic inquiries. We do not sell, monetize, or distribute personal visitor records to commercial third-party marketing entities.

3. Log Files & Analytics Telemetry

CosmicDialDune follows standard industry practices regarding server log files. We employ privacy-conscious analytics services such as Google Analytics (Google Tag ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) to evaluate anonymous reader traffic, page load performance, and browser compatibility across desktop and mobile devices. These metrics are processed in aggregate form and do not link to personally identifiable identities.

4. Cookies & Browser Local Storage

CosmicDialDune utilizes modern browser cookies and HTML5 Local Storage tokens. These small files are saved on your client device to preserve your preferred user interface configuration—such as your chosen visual theme (Midnight Cosmos, Desert Twilight, or Dawn Sand) and in-article typography sizing. You maintain complete control to disable, clear, or refuse cookies at any time through your personal browser settings.

5. GDPR & CCPA Privacy Entitlements

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), all visitors possess statutory data rights. You retain the right to request copies of any personal correspondence records stored in our systems, request corrections to inaccurate records, request complete data deletion, or object to specific data processing activities. To exercise any legal data protection entitlements, please submit a formal request to our privacy desk.
